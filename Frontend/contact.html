THE ESSENTIALS

A few things, answered.

The details that make your ROHENI experience a little easier.

How can I place an order?

Explore our collection, select the piece you love, choose the available options and follow the checkout process to complete your order. If you need help placing an order, get in touch with our team.

Where do you deliver, and how long does it take?

Delivery availability and estimated timelines can vary by destination. Please contact us with your location and we’ll help you confirm the available delivery options.

How can I track my order?

If you need an update on your order, email us with your order number and we’ll help you check its status.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Return and exchange eligibility depends on ROHENI’s current policy. Please contact us before sending an item back so we can guide you through the applicable process.

How do I find the right size?

Check the sizing information provided on the product page before ordering. If you’re unsure about the fit, reach out with the piece you’re interested in and we’ll do our best to help.

How should I care for my ROHENI pieces?

Follow the care label on your garment, as instructions may differ between fabrics and finishes. When in doubt, use the care guidance provided with the specific piece.
