THE PERSON BEHIND ROHENI

I had something to say. I got dressed.

THE BEGINNING

A way to be understood.

I’m Rita, the founder of ROHENI. I’m a creative, and clothes have always been one of the ways I make myself understood. Some things I struggle to put into words come quite naturally through what I wear.

I know what it feels like to be overlooked. To have something in you that other people haven’t quite seen yet. Maybe that’s why I’m drawn to unusual pieces and materials someone else has put aside. I want to spend a little more time with them. See what’s there.

THE JOURNEY

Making something worth keeping.

Back in my prime, I started giving myself more room to experiment. People noticed how I dressed, and “you should start a brand” became a familiar suggestion. In 2024, I decided to see where it could go.

ROHENI grew from that curiosity, my love of thrifting and a growing discomfort with how easily we discard things. Sustainability became part of the way I wanted to create: work with what already exists, pay attention to what’s left over and make something worth keeping.

I came into this with a particular taste and plenty to learn. I’m still learning, alongside the people who help bring these ideas to life.

What I keep coming back to is the feeling of putting on something that makes you feel more like yourself.

That’s what I want to make room for with ROHENI.

WHAT GUIDES US

The way we think.

The ideas behind what we make, how we make it, and what we hope it means to the people who wear it.

Use your imagination.

A small offcut can change a pocket. Two unlikely colours can make a jacket. We leave room to find out.

Be particular.

We care about where a seam lands, how a pocket sits and what happens when you actually put the piece on.

Say what’s in it.

We want you to understand the materials you’re wearing. That means being clear about what is reclaimed, what is newly purchased and what we are still improving.

04 ↗

Leave room for the wearer.

We’ll make the clothes. You’ll have your own ideas about them.

Think beyond the first outing.
